A recoverable, auditable platform connecting job APIs, distributed ASR, long-recording analysis, report generation, and business callbacks.

Public disclosure

Enterprise case study. Only architecture patterns and metrics stated in the current public CV are disclosed.

01

Problem

Large-scale recordings had to move reliably from ingestion through speech recognition and semantic analysis to reports and callbacks, while remaining recoverable and auditable.

02

Challenge

01

High monthly workload and peak-day capacity requirements

02

Long-running stages across GPU nodes and external storage

03

Duplicate messages, partial failure, retry, and state recovery

04

GPU idle time caused by uneven stage throughput

03

Approach

01

Built a LangGraph and PostgreSQL job state machine for submission, transcript validation, windowed analysis, aggregation, reporting, and callbacks.

02

Decoupled preprocessing, FunASR, CAM++, punctuation recovery, and postprocessing through RocketMQ, object storage, and worker pools.

03

Used Inbox and Outbox patterns, idempotent consumers, worker leases, retries, and a dead-letter queue for recoverable execution.

04

Refactored chunk processing into a streaming producer-consumer flow with dynamic batching, stage parallelism, and role-based resource allocation.

06

Key technical decisions

D1

Why a persistent state machine?

Long-running stages needed resumability, observable state, and consistent callback behavior across partial failures.

D2

Why Inbox and Outbox?

At-least-once message delivery required explicit deduplication and reliable coordination between database state and events.

D3

Why producer-consumer streaming?

Feeding downstream GPU stages incrementally reduced idle time and avoided waiting for an entire preprocessing batch.
