Research project / Westlake Lingxi / Westlake University CenBRAIN Lab

sEEG-to-Text Cross-modal Decoding

A two-stage framework that aligns clinical sEEG representations with Chinese text and generates text through an autoregressive decoder.

sEEGMultimodal LearningNeural Decoding
TypeResearch project
Period2025-06 — 2025-09
0.403BLEU-4 on one unseen subject
LoRA r=8parameter-efficient SFT
Public disclosure

Clinical data, subject information, internal code, and unapproved generation samples are not disclosed.

01

Research problem

Clinical sEEG is scarce and strongly subject-dependent, while continuous neural signals and discrete language have a substantial modality gap.

02

Method

01

Built a CNN–Transformer encoder to learn spatiotemporal sEEG representations.

02

Used a frozen BART-Chinese encoder with InfoNCE and random masked reconstruction to align brain and text representations.

03

Connected a BART-Chinese decoder through cross-attention and applied LoRA rank 8 for generative SFT.

03
