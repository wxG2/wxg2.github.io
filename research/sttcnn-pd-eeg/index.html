STTCNN for Parkinson’s EEG Classification

A topology-aware network combining multiscale spatial convolution and period-sensitive temporal modeling for Parkinson’s disease EEG classification.

Research problem

EEG combines electrode topology, short-term dynamics, and long-period patterns that single-scale or single-domain models struggle to model jointly.

02

Method

01

Reordered channel sequences according to scalp-electrode topology.

02

Used progressive multiscale convolution to capture local-to-global spatial interactions.

03

Combined period-sensitive temporal modeling with LSTM-based spatiotemporal integration.
